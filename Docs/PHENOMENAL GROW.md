# **PHENOMENAL GROW**

## **V1 Product Requirements Document**

**Product:** Phenomenal Grow  
**Version:** V1  
**Product Category:** WhatsApp Conversation & Relationship CRM  
**Primary Positioning:** Turn Conversations Into Growth

---

# **1\. PRODUCT VISION**

Phenomenal Grow is a simple, action-focused CRM that helps individuals, businesses, churches and organisations turn WhatsApp conversations into organised relationships, consistent follow-up, conversions and long-term engagement.

The product is built around one fundamental problem:

> **People start conversations, but organisations lose opportunities because they fail to capture, organise, follow up and act consistently.**

Phenomenal Grow ensures that important conversations do not disappear into WhatsApp.

The core journey is:

> **Capture → Understand → Act → Follow Up → Convert → Retain → Re-engage**

The product should always help the user answer five questions:

1. **Who is this person?**  
2. **Where did they come from?**  
3. **What is happening with them?**  
4. **What should happen next?**  
5. **Did the relationship produce an outcome?**

---

# **2\. PRODUCT POSITIONING**

Phenomenal Grow should not be positioned simply as another WhatsApp CRM.

The stronger positioning is:

> **Phenomenal Grow — Turn Conversations Into Growth.**

Supporting promise:

> **Never lose the conversation.**

Phenomenal Grow is not primarily a database.

It is an **action and relationship management system built around conversations**.

# **4\. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES**

## **4.1 Actions Over Administration**

The product should tell users:

> **Who needs me right now?**

rather than simply displaying a database.

---

## **4.2 One Contact \= One Relationship Record**

A person should not become a duplicate Contact every time they start another conversation.

Their history remains connected to one Contact record.

---

## **4.3 Every Active Contact Has a Next Action**

An active Contact should have a clear next step wherever appropriate.

Examples:

* Reply  
* Call  
* Follow up  
* Schedule appointment  
* Send information  
* Move stage  
* Start sequence  
* Re-engage

---

## **4.4 Simple on the Surface, Powerful Underneath**

Phenomenal Grow should be easy for a first-time user while still supporting sophisticated workflows.

Users should not need to understand CRM concepts before they can use the product.

---

## **4.5 Guided Customisation**

Users should be able to customise pipelines, stages, tags, fields and sequences.

However, Phenomenal Grow should provide sensible starting structures rather than presenting users with a completely blank system.

---

## **4.6 Human Relationships First**

Automation should support relationships, not replace them.

Automated follow-ups should pause or stop when human interaction or a defined outcome occurs.

# **9\. CONTACT STATUS**

System statuses remain fixed.

## **Active**

The Contact is currently part of an active relationship or workflow.

## **Inactive**

There is no current action required, but the relationship remains relevant.

## **Archived**

The Contact is removed from the active workspace but remains searchable with their history.

## **Do Not Contact**

The Contact has requested that communication stop.

This status overrides automated follow-up.

### **Do Not Contact behaviour**

When activated:

* Active sequences stop  
* Automated follow-ups stop  
* The Contact remains in the system  
* Conversation history remains available  
* The status is clearly visible

---

# **10\. CONTACT REACTIVATION**

If an archived or inactive Contact starts a new conversation, Phenomenal Grow should be able to bring them back into the active workflow.

The existing history remains intact.

The system should not create a new Contact.

# **43\. PRIMARY USER JOURNEY**

The ideal Phenomenal Grow experience is:

### **STEP 1 — Capture**

A person starts a WhatsApp conversation or submits a Contact Capture form.

↓

### **STEP 2 — Identify**

Phenomenal Grow creates or identifies the Contact.

↓

### **STEP 3 — Attribute**

The system records the available source.

Example:

> Instagram → Campaign → WhatsApp

↓

### **STEP 4 — Assign**

The Contact is assigned to the appropriate Agent.

↓

### **STEP 5 — Place in Pipeline**

The Contact enters the appropriate journey.

↓

### **STEP 6 — Act**

The Agent responds or completes the next action.

↓

### **STEP 7 — Follow Up**

Manual or automated follow-up continues the relationship.

↓

### **STEP 8 — Appointment / Engagement**

If appropriate, an appointment or other milestone is created.

↓

### **STEP 9 — Convert**

The Contact reaches the defined outcome.

↓

### **STEP 10 — Continue**

A post-conversion journey begins.

↓

### **STEP 11 — Retain / Re-engage**

The relationship continues or can be reactivated later.

# **48\. FINAL PRODUCT DEFINITION**

**Phenomenal Grow is an action-focused WhatsApp relationship CRM for individuals, businesses, churches and organisations.**

It captures Contacts from conversations and other entry points, preserves their relationship history, tracks their journey through custom pipelines, assigns responsibility, creates next actions, automates personalised follow-up sequences, manages lightweight appointments, measures outcomes and supports long-term retention and re-engagement.

Its fundamental promise is:

> **Turn Conversations Into Growth.**

And its operating principle is:

> **Never lose the conversation.**
